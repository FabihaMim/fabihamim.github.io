Poster · GSA South-Central Section 2025

Operational Flood Hazard Mapping and Impact Assessment in Bangladesh

An operational approach to mapping flood extent and affected cropland and population from 2020 to 2024, using Sentinel-1, MODIS and Google Earth Engine. The 2020 flood was the most severe, affecting 499,820 hectares and displacing over 4 million people.

Fabiha Shafi Mim and Mohamed H. Aly, University of Arkansas

  • Sentinel-1
  • MODIS
  • Google Earth Engine

Poster · SWAAG Conference 2025

Landsat Study of Land Surface Temperature Dynamics in Texas

Urban heating in Houston, San Antonio and Dallas from 2020 to 2024, using Landsat 8 and 9 imagery, a 5 by 5 km fishnet grid and regression. Dallas had the highest summer land surface temperature, 47.5 °C in 2022.

Fabiha Shafi Mim and Mohamed H. Aly, University of Arkansas

  • Landsat 8 and 9
  • Land surface temperature
  • NDVI and NDBI
  • Regression
